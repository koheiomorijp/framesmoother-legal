Slow motion from a normal-speed video, without the stutter

Slow a 30fps clip down 4x and only 7.5 different pictures are left for each second of playback. If nothing new is made, each picture is simply held longer and the motion moves in steps. FrameSmoother's Slow Motion setting (2x, 4x or 8x) generates the in-between frames on the iPhone instead, so the slowed clip still plays at the frame rate you choose.

03On the iPhone

Slowing a clip down in FrameSmoother

  1. Home

    Tap Select Video and pick the clip. Choose a single video if you want to trim it.

  2. Interpolation Setup › Output Frame Rate

    Keep Target fps and choose 60fps.

  3. Slow Motion

    Pick 2x, 4x or 8x.

  4. Trim

    Move Start and End around the moment you want. Only that part is processed, which also cuts the processing time.

  5. top right › Queue

    Tap Add to Queue. When the row says Completed, tap it: the preview runs the original at the matching slowed position, so both sides show the same moment. Back in the queue, save it with the download-arrow button on the row, or share it with the share button.
