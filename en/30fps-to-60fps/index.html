30fps to 60fps on iPhone, compared frame by frame

A 30fps video changes picture every 33.3 ms. To play at 60fps, a new picture has to exist halfway between every pair of frames. FrameSmoother computes that picture on the iPhone with an AI frame-interpolation model, reuses the original frames between the new ones, and saves a 60fps video. Nothing is uploaded.

03On the iPhone

Making a 60fps copy in FrameSmoother

  1. Home

    Tap Select Video and pick one or more videos from your library.

  2. Interpolation Setup › Output Frame Rate

    Leave Target fps selected and choose 60fps. The line underneath shows what you will get, for example Output: 60 fps.

  3. Interpolation Quality

    High Quality uses the AI model. Fast blends neighbouring frames without AI and finishes sooner.

  4. top right

    Tap Add to Queue. The Queue opens and shows the progress and the estimated time left.

  5. Queue

    When the row says Completed, tap it to open the preview: drag the divider to compare before and after, or step one frame at a time. Back in the queue, save it with the download-arrow button on the row, or share it with the share button (AirDrop and other apps).
